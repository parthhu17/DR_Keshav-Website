FAQ

Questions parents often ask.

Find quick answers about consultations, vaccinations and child healthcare.

What age group does Dr. Keshav treat?

Dr. Keshav provides paediatric consultation for newborns, children and adolescents up to 18 years of age.

Does the clinic provide vaccination services?

Vaccination and immunization services are available for children, including routine childhood vaccinations.

Are newborn consultations available?

Newborn care, well-baby check-ups, growth assessment and breastfeeding counselling are among the listed services.

Is neonatal and NICU care available?

Neonatal and NICU-related services listed include high-risk neonatal care, NICU warmer, phototherapy, CPAP support, ventilator support and central oxygen system.
